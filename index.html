Hosted by Javaresh, we invite you to embrace slow living. Relax in our lush courtyards, enjoy authentic local flavors, and create memories by the Arabian Sea.

Explore Kundapura

Attractions Near Kodi Beach

From tranquil sea walks to historic temples, discover the best of the coast just moments from The Red Floor.

Kodi Sea Walk & Delta Point

A stunning pedestrian walkway where the river meets the Arabian Sea. Perfect for evening strolls, sunset views, and dolphin spotting.

0.5 km (5 min walk)

Kodi Lighthouse

Historic lighthouse offering panoramic views of the coastline and backwaters. A must-visit for photography enthusiasts.

1 km down the coast

Maravanthe Beach

Famous scenic highway where the Suparnika River flows on one side and the Arabian Sea on the other. breathtaking drive.

15-20 min drive North

Backwater Mangrove Forests

Explore the serene beauty of the Kodi backwaters via local boat rides. Ideal for bird watching, kayaking, and peaceful nature photography.

Accessible near Delta Point

Anegudde Vinayaka Temple

One of the most ancient and revered temples in the region, dedicated to Lord Ganesha, situated on a small hillock.

15 min drive inland
